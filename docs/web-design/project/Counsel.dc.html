Item 1 · The French request for fighter squadrons

THE PRIME MINISTER proposed that four squadrons of 12 Group be sent, and no more.

LORD HALIFAX said the French would read four as a refusal. Better to send six and say nothing about limits.

MR CHAMBERLAIN agreed with the Prime Minister. The Air Staff figures were not in doubt.

THE PRIME MINISTER asked the Lord Privy Seal for his view.
