Prime Minister,

Operation Dynamo closed at 02:23 this morning. The Admiralty's figure is 338,226 men landed since 26 May, roughly a third of them French. We have the army. We do not have its guns, its transport, or most of its rifles.

Success Result ofOrder 5·1 · Call out the little ships

The Purchasing Commission has placed the first of its orders. The Americans will sell for cash, and ask that we carry the aircraft away ourselves.

In progress · 2 of 6 FromOrder 5·2 · Fund the Purchasing Commission

Rome radio announces that Signor Mussolini will speak on Monday. Our people in Rome read it as a declaration. I have nothing firmer than that.

Cabinet unity has slipped. The Foreign Secretary let it be known on Thursday that he regards your line on negotiation as premature. He has not resigned and does not intend to.

Needs an answer {{deadline}}

The French ask for ten more fighter squadrons. Send them, or hold them for the defence of Britain?

Air Chief Marshal Dowding has asked to see you before you answer. Three ways you might go:

Send four squadrons A gesture to Reynaud that keeps Fighter Command above Dowding's floor.
Send none, and tell Reynaud plainly Every fighter stays at home. Paris will take it badly, and say so.
Offer a union of the two nations A political answer to a military request. The Cabinet is divided on it.

An open turn. What would you like to start?

Nobody is asking anything of you this week, Prime Minister. That is rare and won't last. Three things I'd consider:

Press Washington for destroyers The Purchasing Commission has a foot in the door. Fifty old destroyers would cover the convoys.
Ready the country for invasion Volunteers, beach defences, the signposts down. The public would rather be busy than wait.
Send Cripps to Moscow Stalin is no friend of ours. Nor, yet, is he Hitler's ally in anything but paper.
