REVISIONIST Interface system · Round 1 Base 1440 × 900 · checked at 1280 × 800 and 1920 × 1080

Two layers: a bronze frame and a paper war room.

The frame (black, matte bronze) is Revisionist itself: header, outer margin, account screens. Everything that belongs to a campaign sits inside it on paper and ink, like a 1940s cabinet office. Dispatches and the agent's voice use the typewriter. Buttons, errors, settings and numbers stay plain IBM Plex Sans.

Rules these components never break
  1. Everything comes from the seat's own view. Unseen means absent.
  2. A false fact looks identical to a true one: no badge, colour, order or tooltip differs.
  3. Other seats are characters only. Nothing shows human, AI or deputy.
  4. Odds are bands, after filing, never percentages.
  5. Rival goals show only the pieces uncovered.
  6. Visibility and certainty always carry an icon and a label.
