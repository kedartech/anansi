Mandate kinds

OrderFrom a superior or governing body. Met: pays means. Ignored: standing with the issuer falls. CrisisFrom the state of the world. Met: damage avoided. Ignored: it runs its course. BlowbackFrom your own earlier action, always linked to it. Met: damage limited. OpportunityA window opens. Met: an advantage. Ignored: no cost.
